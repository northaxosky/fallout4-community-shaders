# `shaders/lighting/`

Reconstructed HLSL reference for FO4's deferred lighting pipeline.

This directory is a **reference**, not a runtime asset. Files here are
human-readable reconstructions of Bethesda's precompiled `.fxp` shaders
intended to inform feature implementations elsewhere in the repo.

## Phase A reconstruction status

* **`ambient_ibl_pass.hlsl`** — **renderdoc-confirmed, HLSL still WIP**.
  All structural questions are now answered (see `shader-3560-analysis.md`):
  * **AO-application boundary** (line-264 single multiply on the
    combined ambient+IBL term, no direct-light contamination).
  * **kSSAO write timeline** (Phase B): SAO writes at
    `Render_PreUI +0x036b`; ambient PS reads at `+0x039c`. Hook point:
    `RegisterPreDeferredLightsImpl` (already implemented by you).
  * **SRV map (Phase C)**: 14 SRVs identified by RenderDoc capture
    `FO4_frame5407.rdc` event-id 45345. High-confidence: t1=kGbufferNormal,
    t4=kGbufferAlbedo, t7=main depth, t8=IBL probe cubemap array,
    t9=kSSAO, t14=kMainPreAlpha (lit scene), t15=kMainDepthMips.
    Medium-confidence: t2/t3=gbuffer aux, t5/t11=ambient diffuse pair,
    t6/t10/t12=screen-space ambient HDR scratch.
  * **Output**: o0 = kDiffuseBuffer (R11G11B10F) confirmed.
  * **Remaining gap**: literal HLSL round-trip. The 177-instruction
    bilateral SSSS-style blur (ASM lines 61-238) needs a multi-iteration
    HLSL → DXC → diff loop that exceeds the runbook's single-pass
    budget. CB byte contents are dumped and available for offline
    parsing; only the named struct mapping is pending.
* **`deferred_composite.hlsl`** — **WIP, blob misidentified** (2026-05-18
  campaign). The Phase-A classifier picked blob 2122 as the canonical
  deferred-composite PS, but disassembly proves it is actually a
  per-light deferred geometry pass (per-vertex inputs, sun BRDF math, no
  albedo SRV). Blob 556 (the dominant-shape representative) is also
  misidentified — it is a particle/decal shader. The real composite PS
  fires at RenderDoc capture eid 45368 (sha1 prefix `813c9acec23b`,
  6 SRVs, 2 CBs, writes RT 172 = kMain) but that bytecode is **not**
  packed in `Shaders011.fxp` or embedded in `Fallout4.exe`. See
  `shader-2122-analysis.md` and
  `docs/lighting-shader-followups.md` §"Shaders011.2122" for the open
  items.
* **`directional_sun_light.hlsl`** — **WIP, permutation uncertain** (2026-05-18
  campaign). The Phase-A classifier picked blob 3147 (sha1 prefix
  `a5e2f8a0985e`, 904 instructions, MRT) as the canonical sun-light PS.
  Three-step verification: input signature is fullscreen-quad-shaped
  (PASS), MRT to `o0`+`o1` (PASS), but live-capture cross-check
  **FAILS** — the captured sun-shadow draws (eids 45401-45623) use a
  completely different PS (`46b911cb8053`, srv=1 cb=4) that is **not in
  the on-disk corpus**. 3147 belongs to a 30+ shape-identical peer
  cluster in `Shaders011.fxp`; no evidence singles out any specific
  peer as canonical. See `shader-3147-analysis.md` and
  `shader-corpus-survey.md` (which generalises the corpus gap to the
  entire deferred-lighting pipeline).

## Phase A candidate disassemblies

`Fallout4 - Shaders.ba2` has been extracted and indexed. Every PS in the
3,939-blob shader pack has been disassembled and classified by its
resource-binding shape. Top candidates per role
(see `Fallout4RE/Scratch/reports/phase-a-shader-candidates.json`):

* **directional-sun-light-MRT** - 197 permutations (117 unique sha1).
  Hottest representative: `Shaders011.3147` (904 instructions, 82
  sample calls, 6 SRVs incl. comparison-mode sampler for shadow PCF, 2
  CBs, MRT to o0+o1). Dominant shape bucket: cb=2 srv=5 sampler=5 out=2
  with 141 permutations spanning 120-339 instructions.
* **ambient-or-ibl** - 194 permutations (128 unique). Hottest: blob
  `3560` (321 insns, 14 SRVs, 3 CBs, includes a texturecube SRV for
  the environment). Dominant bucket: cb=2 srv=5 sampler=5 with 60
  permutations spanning 51-257 instructions.
* **deferred-composite** - 236 permutations (119 unique). Hottest:
  blob `2122` (106 insns, 3 SRVs, 3 CBs). Dominant bucket: cb=3 srv=3
  sampler=2 with 138 permutations spanning 42-90 instructions.

Disassembled asm files live at
`Fallout4RE/Scratch/shaders-extracted/ShadersFX/index/Shaders011/asm/Shaders011.<blob>.<sha1[:12]>.dxbc.asm`.
The classifications are based on resource shape only; the manual
reconstruction step (per the runbook) maps each SRV slot to a render-
target index by reading the dispatch site's `SetShaderResources` C++
code.

## Workflow

The reconstruction pipeline is documented in
`Fallout4RE/Workspace/docs/lighting-shader-reconstruction-runbook.md`.
At a high level:

1. **Mechanical stage** (automated, no judgement required):
   * Extract `Data/Fallout4 - Shaders.ba2` with BSArch, then run
     `python Fallout4RE/Workspace/tools/commands/index-fxp-shaders.py
     <Shaders011.fxp> --write-asm-for-phase-a` to disassemble and
     index every blob.
   * Run `python Fallout4RE/Workspace/tools/commands/classify-phase-a-shader-candidates.py`
     to surface the candidate clusters.
2. **Manual stage** (per-shader; honest reconstruction):
   * Pick the hottest representative from the dominant shape bucket of
     each role.
   * For each target asm, identify CB layout, texture slots, and BRDF
     shape. Use `// TODO: identify` rather than guessing.
   * Cross-check by recompiling the reconstruction with `dxc.exe` and
     diffing the resulting DXBC against the original ASM.

The runbook bars speculation: any HLSL committed here is expected to
either (a) round-trip to ASM that closely matches the original bytecode,
or (b) be marked WIP with explicit `// TODO` blocks.

## Why these three first

Phase A targets are chosen so that delivering them unblocks SSGI Phase 2
in the sibling integration: the ambient/IBL pass tells us how the engine
applies AO to the ambient term, so the SSGI feature can integrate at the
right boundary instead of post-modulating `kDiffuseBuffer` (which
darkens direct light along with ambient).

Phase B (point/spot lights, shadow application, fog, material
permutations) is gated on Phase A delivering at least one verified
reconstruction.
