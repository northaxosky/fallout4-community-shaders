# Skylighting

| Kind | Upstream | FO4 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forced | Rain occlusion mask replaced by the Skylighting capture | Capture runs after the stock rain pass into an owned 512x512 depth; stock rain keeps its own mask | The stock mask must stay intact |
| Forced | Occluders follow the stock render-pass list | An owned utility pass covers occluders stock rejects: loaded-cell landscape, geometry without Cast Shadows, alpha-blended geometry, faded materials | The stock rain-mask pass list excludes them |
| Divergence | Occluders follow the stock rain-mask gather | The gather seeds the culler with the fade nodes and geometry whose bounds reach the capture frustum, pruning subtrees outside it, instead of whole cells; objects the culler treats as fully faded out (fade amount 0) no longer occlude | The stock fallback walk registers every object of each reached cell, which costs CPU in dense exteriors |
| Divergence | Occluders are drawn by the engine, one draw each | Opaque static occluders (plain, mesh LOD, landscape, merge-instanced) are recorded and drawn in four batched calls that pull vertices from the engine's pooled geometry heaps, bit-identical to the engine's depth; alpha-tested, tree-animated and not-yet-uploaded geometry stays on the engine path. `BatchOccluders` turns it off | The engine issues about 2,500 CPU draws per capture in dense exteriors |
| Forced | Water specular reads `input.WPosition` and the probe array | Water binds the probe array and replaces the constant that fed the cube lerp with probe specular visibility | FO4 water has no world-position input |
| N/A | Water ambient scaled by `skylightingDiffuse` | | FO4 water ambient is a sun lobe independent of sky or DALC |
| Forced | `ApplySkylighting` subtracts ambient, rescales and adds it back | Multiplies the ambient addend by `MultiBounceAO(albedo, diffuse visibility)`; upstream's over-subtraction (ambient removed that was never added for ENVMAP, multi-layer parallax, eye, LOD-land PBR) is not reproduced | FO4 ambient is a separate linear addend |
| Forced | `skylightingDiffuse` and ambient specular share `directionalAmbientColor` | Diffuse (normal) and specular (reflection direction) ambient take the same per-pixel factor in the DFLight and tiled families; `MinSpecularVisibility` does not reach it | Ambient is evaluated per family before local lights accumulate |
| Forced | `vertexAO` from vertex color in the lighting pixel shader | `1 - vertexAO` is stored in MRT4 alpha by the prepass and shared with [Screen Space GI](ScreenSpaceGI.md) | No separate vertex-AO channel exists |
| Forced | Grass albedo takes the normalized vertex color | Same, as a uniform branch on the Skylighting/SSGI enable flags; disabled features keep stock albedo | Prepass grass permutations |
| Forced | `DeferredCompositeCS` separates ambient by `Masks.z` | `ComposeDiffuse` scales its recomputed ambient by the same visibility factor; only in composite variants that run SSGI | See [Screen Space GI](ScreenSpaceGI.md) |
| Forced | Effect ambient scaled by `skylightingDiffuse` | Only lit effects sample; the ambient part is the ambient split of `DLightColor`, small in exteriors | FO4 effect lighting |
| Forced | Shadow cascades snooped from the bound shadow SRV | Cascades are copied from the sun pass into an owned array; the probe update samples the previous frame's cascades. Supports 1-3 cascades (Skyrim assumes 2); an invalid matrix leaves every probe lit | The stock array is overwritten after the sun pass |
| Forced | `SKYLIGHTING_SHADOW_VIS` on rim/soft/back lighting | Soft-wrap, rim and back lobes use the shadow-visibility volume instead of the cascade shadow; direct lobe and specular keep the cascade shadow. Only sun lobes of directional families are affected | |
| Forced | Dynamic Cubemaps specular lerps env and reflections cubes by `skylightingSpecular`, weighted by DALC at the reflection vector | Composite probe sites lerp the two cubes by probe specular visibility; native probe specular weight (`ambient + sun + local diffuse`) replaces the DALC weight | The DALC term cannot be separated from the native sum, and no reflectance channel exists |
| Pending | b6 zeroed while the map menu is open | Unresolved whether the FO4 local map needs it | |
